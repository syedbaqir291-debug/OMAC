Mission, Vision & Core Values

More About OMAC →

Mission

To make patient safety and healthcare quality measurable, teachable, and built into everyday operations — through practical training, original research, and purpose-built digital tools that hospitals and quality professionals can use immediately, not just during survey season.

Vision

To be a leading independent voice in healthcare quality and patient safety around the globe — where OMAC's frameworks, tools, and training are a default reference point for quality professionals worldwide, grounded in evidence rather than opinion.

01

Evidence Over Opinion

Every framework and recommendation is traceable to data, standards, or peer-reviewed reasoning.

02

Built Inside Real Systems

Solutions designed from hands-on hospital experience — not generic consulting templates.

03

Patient Safety First

Quality work exists to protect patients and staff — passing an audit is a byproduct, not the goal.

04

Continuous Improvement

A Kaizen mindset applied to OMAC's own tools and knowledge base, not just client systems.

05

Open Access to Knowledge

Free tools, learning modules, and research made publicly available to raise the standard across the profession.
