Enterprise Build

Get a Customized Incident Reporting Module — built for your organization.

The OMAC Integrated Incident Reporting System (IRS) is a complete closed-loop platform — from the moment an incident is reported to the moment the loop is closed and the trend data feeds back into continuous improvement. It's configured end-to-end around your facility's roles, categories, and escalation paths.

Step 01

User Reporting

Simple, quick, and confidential incident submission for staff and patients — objective description, affected type, and affected person, submitted in seconds.

Step 02

Verification & Triaging

Quality representatives review, verify, and triage each report with built-in analysis tools, automatically creating a registry entry.

Step 03

Assignment & Investigation

Department heads receive assigned cases, conduct investigations, log findings, and assign specific tasks — tracked on their own dashboard.

Step 04

Loop Closure & Improvement

Root cause analysis and action plans feed back into the registry — closing the loop and turning incidents into quality and safety trend data.

Complete Closed-Loop

Every incident moves through reporting, triage, investigation, and closure — nothing falls through the cracks.

Role-Based Dashboards

Separate views for reporters, quality representatives, and HoDs — each sees only what's relevant to their role.

Summary Analytics

Total incidents, pending investigations, closed loops, and risk levels — all on one live summary dashboard.

Built for Your Workflow

Categories, roles, and thresholds configured to match how your organization actually operates — not a rigid template.
