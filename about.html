Who We Are

About OMAC International

OMAC International (also known as OMAC by S M Baqir, or OMAC Developers) is a healthcare quality consultancy and digital tools brand founded by Syed Muhammad Baqir in Lahore, Pakistan. It grew from a single portfolio site into a portfolio of 30+ tools and platforms spanning incident reporting, learning management systems, KPI analytics, Quality Intelligence Dahsboards, CPHQ exam preparation, and quality reference libraries — all aimed at one problem: healthcare quality work is too often manual, siloed, and rebuilt from scratch by every hospital. OMAC exists to fix that with practical training, published research, and purpose-built software.

OMAC operates across three lines of work: Training (healthcare quality assurance, data analytics, and communication skills for hospital teams), Quality Automation (automating audit trails, compliance tracking, and KPI reporting), and Enterprise Digital Solutions (intelligence dashboards, digital QMS, training portals, and incident reporting systems built around a facility's actual workflow) — alongside a growing set of free tools and learning modules for the wider quality community.

What Drives OMAC

Mission, Vision & Core Values

Mission

To make patient safety and healthcare quality measurable, teachable, and built into everyday operations — through practical training, original research, and purpose-built digital tools that hospitals and quality professionals can use immediately, not just during survey season.

Vision

To be a leading independent voice in healthcare quality and patient safety around the globe — where OMAC's frameworks, tools, and training are a default reference point for quality professionals worldwide, grounded in evidence rather than opinion.

01

Evidence Over Opinion

Every framework and recommendation is traceable to data, standards, or peer-reviewed reasoning — reflected in OMAC's OAPR and SPC research.

02

Built Inside Real Systems

Solutions designed from hands-on hospital experience across JCI, PHC, and SHC surveys — not generic consulting templates.

03

Patient Safety First

Quality work exists to protect patients and staff — passing an audit is a byproduct, not the goal.

04

Continuous Improvement

A Kaizen mindset applied to OMAC's own tools and knowledge base, not just client systems.

05

Open Access to Knowledge

Free tools, learning modules, and research made publicly available to raise the standard across the profession.

Founder

Syed Muhammad Baqir

MPhil TQM · CQAP · Six Sigma Black Belt · CPHQ (in progress)

Syed Muhammad Baqir is a healthcare quality and patient safety professional with 8+ years of overall experience, including 6+ years specifically in Healthcare Quality and Patient Safety across the public and private sector — covering JCI, Healthcare Commission, Mock Tracer, and Audit work. He has covered two JCI audits under the 8th edition survey and one JCI ambulatory audit survey, and carries deep expertise in the JCI 7th-to-8th edition transition, ISO 9001:2015, CAP, and statistical process control.

Within Pakistan's healthcare quality community, Baqir has increasingly been referred to as a Quality Guru — a reflection of his original research output (SPC methodologies like the Objective Astronomical Point Rule, extensions to Garvin's product-quality dimensions and the SERVQUAL/RATER framework, and applied Ishikawa/FMEA critiques), his CPHQ/JCI/ISO training work, and OMAC's growing library of free tools built for the profession rather than for a single client.
